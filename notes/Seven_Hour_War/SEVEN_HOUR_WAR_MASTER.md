## PART 1 — THE CORE CONCEPTION

**The one-paragraph version (CURRENT):**
On the surface the Seven Hour War is an alien invasion: the Combine arrive, overwhelm every army in seven hours, Breen negotiates surrender. Underneath, it is Hell opening onto Earth, the planned culmination of a story God wrote for humanity. The resonance cascade at Black Mesa was the catalyst, but only one event in a causal chain; had it not been the cascade, something else would have arrived at precisely the same time. Between cascade and war come months of widening dimensional instability. Xenofauna infest the planet, wars stop, humanity drops into crisis survival mode, and strange phenomena appear that are religious signs. Then, months later, the seven hours. About 90 percent of humanity dies.

**The two readings:**
- **Secular reading:** physics accident, dimensional rift, interstellar empire exploiting it, negotiated surrender.
- **Real reading (LOCKED, never stated outright):** God of Abraham is real; war is secretly the rapture; Combine are secretly Hell's legions; G-Man is Lucifer. Hell floods Earth, wards off what it hates, leaves what it permits.

**Governing theme (WORKING):**
Violence begets violence until someone chooses to be the last one (Romans 12:21). The Seven Hour War is the largest act of violence in the book. The answer is small: people holding each other.

## PART 2 — HALF-LIFE FOUNDATION (SOURCE)

**What the games establish:**
- Black Mesa Incident opened dimensional rift through resonance cascade.
- Combine (interdimensional empire) used portal storm to reach Earth.
- War lasts seven hours. Name echoes the 1967 Six-Day War.
- Outcome: decisive Combine victory, capitulation of all Earth governments, occupation.
- Dr. Wallace Breen managed Earth's surrender, was rewarded with Administrator post.
- Death toll described as massive, never quantified. **90% figure is author's own, not Half-Life's.**
- After war Combine imposed reproductive suppression field. No new generation for years.
- Humans retreated into cities, nearly went extinct.
- More humans died to Xenian attacks in following years than in war itself.

**Warrior divergence:**
In Half-Life the Combine arrive within days of Black Mesa. **In Warrior, there are months of ever-widening dimensional instability** between cascade and war.

## PART 3 — THE COSMOLOGY (what it really is)

### 3.1 The Plan and the Door

**[CURRENT]** The resonance cascade was planned. One event in a causal chain that plays out the story of humanity as planned by God. If not the cascade, something else would have happened at precisely the same time.

### 3.2 Hell Floods the Earth

**[CURRENT]** As Hell floods over Earth, it wards off everything it hates and leaves what it allows: headcrabs, xenofauna.

**[DERIVED]** This makes the Combine's victory a sorting rather than only a conquest. What belongs to Heaven is driven out; what belongs to the pit is left standing.

**[LOCKED]** The Combine are secretly the legions of Hell. Humanity's own cruelty came first; Combine did not teach it.

### 3.3 The Dominions and Principalities

**[CURRENT]** During the months of instability they appear to people as new forms of alien life. Go away after Combine take over, warded off.

### 3.4 The Vortigaunts

**[CURRENT]** The Vortigaunts are not angels. They are pilgrims of various dimensions of existence. They know God, the devil, and the rest.

**[CURRENT]** People never ask them for religious interpretation, so when Vortigaunts speak of "The Conclusion" or "The Evil One," humans hear only superstition.

**[OUTLINE/DRAFT]** They are present "around the various chapters of the whole story." In finale they recognize Lucifer's attempted transfer into Isaac, combine power to ward him off, call toward the Great Beyond. They name G-Man as Lucifer; at that moment Aage understands invasion was rapture.

### 3.5 The Great Beyond

**[LOCKED]** The Great Beyond is God. Intervenes only after human story is decided. Reads as judgment and closure, not rescue. Represented as presence, pull, light or absence, never speaking character.

### 3.6 Breen and the G-Man / Lucifer

**Breen:** Distant, polite, reasonable, catastrophic. Ended war by managing surrender. After war he initiated into cult preserving Lucifer's blood. Uses Combine medicine to preserve and move it.

**G-Man:** Secretly Lucifer, two states (corporeal avatar in host body; non-corporeal form surviving host death). Possession requires physical blood bridge. Christ's blood sacred and lost; Lucifer's blood its dark mirror.

**Hosts:** Cult leader is Lucifer in dying body. Aage was intended next host; uprising made him unavailable; Aaron Considine becomes substitute.

## PART 4 — THE LONG FUSE: MONTHS BEFORE THE WAR

**[CURRENT]** In Half-Life Combine arrive within days. In Warrior, **months of ever-widening dimensional instability.**

### 4.1 What Happens in Those Months

- Instability lets alien xenofauna infest planet.
- Wars stop. Humanity enters crisis survival mode. Things get darker.
- Many people die to infestation.
- But people finally work together to save each other.
- "Scientific" phenomena appear that are really religious signs: trumpets in sky, Dominions and Principalities as new aliens.
- Culminates months later in Seven Hour War.

### 4.3 The Signs (Catalog)

**CURRENT:** Trumpets in sky mistaken for meteorology; Dominions and Principalities mistaken for aliens.

**COMMITTED additional signs, all deniable as natural phenomena:**
- Weather keeping a rhythm: pressure waves at regular intervals radio hobbyists track.
- Birds leaving region the day before something arrives.
- Instruments agreeing on reading that makes no physical sense.
- Sky holding a color nobody has a name for at dusk.
- Crowds going quiet at same moment in places that cannot hear each other.
- Newspapers explaining each sign with increasingly implausible mechanism.

### 4.4 The Thesis of the Months

**[CURRENT and COMMITTED]**
This is where humanity learns, too late and for first time, how to be good to each other. Should be warm, painful, luminous half of war story.

**[COMMITTED]** The months also kill. Infestation takes large share of dead before Combine ever land.

## PART 5 — THE SEVEN HOURS

**What is established (CURRENT / LOCKED):**
- War begins as Aetheas about to launch nuclear weapons. Sky "thunders open." From window Aetheas sees clouds pouring across land like locusts.
- About 90% of humanity dies.
- Hell wards off what it hates. Dominions and Principalities driven off.
- Many cities cratered, ashen and burning.

### 5.1 Hour-by-Hour Skeleton [COMMITTED]

**Hour 1 — The Opening.** Sky thunders open. The swarm. First minute is wonder before terror.

**Hour 2 — The Wards.** Trumpets stop. Dominions and Principalities gone. Sudden absence felt before understood. Hell has begun flooding Earth.

**Hour 3 — Command Breaks.** Governments lose thread. Orders unanswered.

**Hour 4 — The Cities.** Weight falls on places with most people. Fire, collapse, panic. People who help each other and who don't, side by side.

**Hour 5 — The Last Stands.** Armies, units, households fight with what they have. Where bravery without hope lives.

**Hour 6 — The Voice.** Radio connects survivors to Breen. The offer begins taking shape.

**Hour 7 — The Surrender.** Earth's governments capitulate. War ends not by winning but by saying yes.

## PART 6 — THE AFTERMATH

### 6.1 The First Days

**Aetheas [OUTLINE, adjusted by Lore 11].** Days after war, lone Volkswagen drives through ashen landscape. Aetheas (paralyzed, wheelchair) driven by General Euphem. Radio connects to Breen, who assigns "districts" to hold remnants of humanity. Aetheas receives eastern Mediterranean.

**Aage [OUTLINE].** Hid in basement during apocalypse. Everything shook. Survived remarkably. Came out to find everything on fire. Wandered blasted hellscape among burnt and dismembered dead. Word spreads: go to Larissa.

**The road [OUTLINE].** Men steal from elderly. Burnt corpses of dogs and children. Rivers clogged with bodies. Transported for two days. End of line is Sablino (Russia), then bus to Firebase Lancaster under Captain Judge.

## PART 7 — THE CAST AND THE WAR

**Aage Stronos.** Shoots father at Ch298 as war begins. Runs into ruins. Fear circuitry already broken; war does not frighten him.

**Isaac.** Born at start of Seven Hour War. Parents shelter in windowless apartment bathroom. Building falls. Father throws himself over them: "I love you." Mother curls around him: "My baby." Last word: "Live." Survives in pocket their bodies make, crying from hunger and cold. Firefighter hears and digs for hours.

**Matt.** Same age as Isaac, born in quiet cottage far from war. Ordinary child story spared. Always alive and protected.

**Ezra.** Not yet born; one of first babies after suppression field falls. Isaac and Matt born before field; Ezra first after.

**"Mama" and caregiver house.** After war, Isaac taken to Municipal Children's Relief Annex. Old woman counts children morning and night. When Combine come she hits first soldier with stove iron, is shot twice. Last silent words: "My babies."

**Nina Sokolova and Motherland Regiment.** Survives, becomes human origin of Mother Russia myth.

## Imagery and Motifs

- **The sky:** Big blue sky embodies peace and breadth. During months and war it is wrong.
- **"Come with me."** Justine's phrase, hijacked by Breen, answered at end.
- **Hands and carrying.** Who holds whom. Buried babies, handled hands, stiffened hand moved.
- **Bread and honey.** Hunger, trust, gift.
- **Noise returning.** "We're loud again." Silence is aftermath; noise is life.
- **Flowers in skulls, vines over ruins, seeds beneath ash.**
- **The white lion.** Aage's child-self.
- **The calm dead.** Optional image: bodies look calm, no fear on faces.
