# ISAAC - COMPREHENSIVE CHARACTER DOSSIER, ORPHAN-TO-WARRIOR

Compiled October 25, 2026 from the Warrior Drive files. Status words follow canon hierarchy (LOCKED / CURRENT / WORKING). Every line comes from a file listed in section 25.

## 1. IDENTITY AND AGE

- Name: Isaac. No surname. Named from a dead man's coat after rescue.
- Role: feral survivor who becomes Aage's adopted son. Calls Aage "Dad."
- Ezra is exactly 10 years younger than Isaac (LOCKED).
- Born at the start of the Seven Hour War. 4 days old when apartment block collapses.

**Age markers:**
- 8 at the City 20 uprising
- 10 or 11 at first meeting with Aage
- 11 at the snowball fight, Ezra 1 (LOCKED)
- 13 at the Depot, Ezra 3 (LOCKED)
- 14 in post-Depot recovery scenes
- 16 in the Brush Devil and Old Commander scenes
- about 25 in The Things We Carry
- 18 to 20+ in the City 20 return and courtship

## 2. APPEARANCE THROUGH TIME

- Age 8: shaved head, cut hands, burned wrist, sprained ankle
- Age 9 (road): thin, ribs showing, uneven dark hair, chipped tooth from cold
- Age 13: dark hair, freckles, brown eyes, pale from fever
- Age 16: taller, broader, loud, carries a shotgun
- Adult (~25): broad shoulders, dark hair with first gray, thick wool coat, short beard

**Permanent marks:** burn scar on wrist, scars on both palms

## 3. BIRTH AND THE SEVEN HOUR WAR

- Parents shelter in an apartment bathroom. The building comes down.
- Father throws himself over mother and child: "I love you."
- Mother curls around him: "My baby." Last word: "Live."
- Survives in the pocket their bodies make, crying from hunger and cold.
- Firefighter hears him and digs for hours. Five men from the station work by hand.
- Wrapped in a dead man's coat with "Isaac" stitched on the breast.
- This is the only fully written Seven Hour War scene.

## 4. THE CAREGIVER HOUSE

- Official name: Municipal Children's Relief Annex.
- Run by an old woman (called "mama" by small children).
- She counts the children morning and night, apologizes when she thinks no one hears.
- Ties a ribbon around the wrist of any child who dies.
- Isaac likes being held, the stove, a blue chipped cup, a wooden horse with one leg missing.
- When the Combine come she hits the first soldier with a stove iron and is shot twice.
- Last silent words: "My babies."
- Isaac screams and reaches for her open hand on the floor, touching the blood on his sleeve.

## 5. THE TRAIN TO CITY 20

- Packed train, slatted windows too high to see out, a bucket no one can reach.
- Water comes in a pail thrown into the car. He learns to move sooner than others.
- Arrival at City 20: flat female Overwatch voice, then Dr. Breen welcoming them to "the finest examples of our new civic standard."
- He walks into the city with the old woman's blood still on his sleeve.

## 6. CITY 20 LABOR HOUSE

- Converted school. Children strip wire in a hot workroom with painted white windows.
- Dormitory beds in rows, a bucket at the end, a hissing pipe when it rains.
- Faded painted sun, tree and red-roofed house on the wall from before the war.
- Sealed basement door with a Combine lock lit by blue light.
- Isaac turns mean in the city. Biting is his favorite weapon.
- He beat Tarik bloody over bread.

## 7. ELENA

- A fellow child laborer. They watch trains at sunset and talk about places with trees, too many to count.
- She gives him a small blue button with four holes and one chipped edge.
- Line: "You don't keep things for for."
- He is the only person who knows about "the trees."
- Death: She dies when Isaac, hidden under the bed, lets go of her hand.

## 8. TARIK

- A boy in the labor house, older and harder.
- He kills a supervisor with a metal sorting tray and starts the escape.
- He shoves smaller children through the door and up the stairs.
- On the top floor he faces a poison zombie with a broken pipe.
- Final glimpse: one hand reaching out for anything.

## 9. THE UPRISING (Backstory Chapter 3, Isaac age 8)

**Timeline:**
- Lights blink, loudspeaker announces civic disruption, explosions and gunfire follow.
- Supervisors lose control. Tarik kills one. Isaac bites another. Elena takes his sleeve and they run.
- Civil Protection fires into the crowd. Children flee.
- They run home to the dormitory (the only map they have).
- Combine lock on basement is dead. Zombies and headcrabs come up, followed by a poison zombie.
- Tarik makes his stand at the top floor. Isaac and Elena try to break a painted-shut window and hide under a bed.
- Elena's shoulder catches, poison zombie seizes her ankle.
- Isaac holds her hand with both of his. Her hand slips out finger by finger. She screams his name once.
- He tries to stay silent with both hands over his mouth and fails by one small sound.
- The zombie turns and lifts the bed. He sees Elena headless with fire behind her. The image burns in permanently.
- A burning beam falls on the zombie. He crawls out through fire and glass past Elena's hand.
- Heat strips skin from one side of his wrist. He breaks the window and drops to the alley.
- He stands in the alley calling Elena's name, then Tarik's, waiting. Roof caves in. No one comes out.

## 10. THE TRAIN OUT AND THE LOST BUTTON

- He realizes the button is gone. For a moment that breaks him more than the fire.
- On the packed train he bites a woman who touches him.
- The train stops in open country. Passengers scatter.

## 11. THE ROAD (Backstory Chapter 4)

- No bell, no meal line, no schedule. Freedom makes it worse.
- He steals and leaves. When caught he bites. Adults call him rat, dog, little savage.
- Food: grain, rotten fruit, boiled leather, beetles, worms, bark, grass, marrow, rainwater. First dead headcrab in late autumn under a bridge, eaten while crying.
- He will not enter a basement or cellar. He runs from a farmhouse cellar door.
- Woman at a roadside inn offers soup. He runs because a child laughs inside.
- Near-death in winter: falls through ice, crawls out, digs nails into wrist scar all night to stay awake. Tooth chips.
- Three wolves or dogs track him half a day. He fights on a tractor cab with a rusted knife, wounds the leader, finds it dead later and eats it.
- By spring he is nine. He reaches the hills above the sea.

## 12. ARRIVAL AT THE ORCHARDS

- Stone houses, terraces, olive and fig trees.
- Ahad is the old hard man with the shotgun. Com is the priest and doctor.
- Isaac sees food, not home. He steals and hides it.
- He is fed because he works and watched because he bites.
- He sleeps outside under the wall, will not enter houses, will not go near cellars.
- The "stopping" is the miracle: his body reaches its limit and he stays.
- He eats fallen figs until he vomits, then collapses under a tree.
- Samir and Ahad come. Isaac hisses. Samir tosses bread. Isaac eats and bites Samir's forearm. Samir holds the back of his neck gently. Isaac passes out.
- He wakes under the outer wall with a blanket and a bowl.

## 14. ISAAC AND AAGE: THE ARC IN ORDER

**First encounter, the wolf night (LOCKED):**
- A few weeks after Aage arrives with Ezra. Aage wakes in a panic attack and nearly strangles sleeping Ezra.
- Isaac is at the edge of the orchard when a wolf comes. Aage's arrow hits it.
- Aage comes out of the dark in killer mode. Isaac sees his eyes and knows something is wrong with him.
- An episode is obvious to everyone: Aage's eyes go blank.
- No words pass between them.

**First interaction, the bread fight (LOCKED):**
- Ezra is about 2 months old, asleep on Aage's chest.
- The fight is over bread Aage meant for Ezra. Isaac swings a punch toward Ezra.
- Aage throws him and pins both wrists with the prosthetic.
- Isaac's face goes rage, fear, then shame. He cries and screams "FUCK YOU!", which startles Ezra and snaps Aage out of it.
- Aage lets go, sets the crushed bread on the table and leaves without turning around so Isaac can take it.

**The frosty period:**
- They argue constantly. Isaac sees Aage as terrifying and predatory but also the thing that saved him.

**The snowball fight (LOCKED: Isaac 11, Ezra 1):**
- Isaac confronts Aage angrily after Aage turns down Nadia.
- Aage throws a snowball almost as an afterthought. It becomes real play.
- Isaac laughs, realizes he is happy, gets scared, and runs off crying.
- After this Isaac tacitly stays near Aage.
- Hadi, about 10, is Isaac's rival and later best friend.
- Christmas Eve: community gathers round a pine. Isaac silently sits beside Aage and never mentions the snowball.

**The Depot (Isaac 13, Ezra 3, LOCKED):**
- Com needs surgical equipment. Isaac teaches Ezra to throw a wooden knife, later gives him his own.
- Isaac moves with trained competence. Aage's handprint unlocks the checkpoint.
- Medical wing is a morgue with child-sized shapes. A small child's shoe breaks him.
- A corpse attacks. Isaac freezes because zombies tie to his deepest trauma.
- He half-says, half-pleads "Dad?" before falling. Involuntary; his defenses are stripped.
- Aage shoots with the .357. The shots wake the depot. Aage carries Isaac out repeating "Stay with me."
- Ezra calms the horses, hauls the surgical case onto Aisha alone, mounts alone.
- Surgery: Isaac must be held down by Aage. "Not almost. Not gently." Open vessels cauterized with heated iron.
- Aage calls him "Son" to pull him back. Isaac keeps saying "I'm cold" in a hot room. "Am I home?" "Yes." "Okay."

**The fever and the telling (Epilogue):**
- Three nights of fever. Isaac reaches for Elena, the bed, the button.
- Aage holds him once when memory takes his body, lets him bite a sleeve.
- When lucid Isaac tells Aage everything: train, labor house, basement, stairs, Tarik, Elena, bed, button, road, dead headcrabs, ice, wolves, orchard.
- "I let go." "I had her hand." Aage does not argue him out of it.
- Aage says: "I know what it is to live because someone else did not."
- Aage tells him he carried her as far as a child could carry anyone.
- Isaac: "I don't remember her voice right anymore... I remember the bad part best."
- Aage: "It will still be there. But they stop being the only thing in the room."
- Isaac asks "Am I like them?" Aage says no, because he is ashamed and still knows the difference.
- Recovery: becomes unbearable by day four, which is how they know he is healing.
- Evening: Isaac leans his shoulder against Aage, then says he will deny it.
- At the door he says "Dad?" and then "Come on. It's cold."

## 21. PSYCHOLOGY

**Core guilt:** "I let go." Intellectually knows he was a child; emotionally guilt persists. It is eased, never erased.

**Defenses:** biting, swearing, stealing, refusing help, trying to look dangerous instead of afraid. Ashamed of being seen afraid.

**Triggers:**
- zombies, headcrabs, cellars, basements
- beds being lifted
- anything moving slowly in the dark
- wet cough in the dark
- small child's shoe
- smoke from cooking fire
- raised voices
- his body freezes and he cannot act

**Habits that persist for years:** hides food, eats too fast, sleeps with back to wall, hands over mouth during nightmares, reaches for a knife.

**What changes over time:** biting, hiding food, flinching from touch, refusing comfort, saying nothing about love. By 14 he can say "I love you." By 16 he steps in to stop Aage. By adulthood he cries openly and speaks about Elena.

**What does not change:** swearing, argument, teasing, stubbornness, pride, need to hide fear behind a joke.

## 22. VOICE AND MANNERISMS

- Profane and sarcastic: "Fuck off," "old man," "stubborn old bastard," "Jesus Christ."
- Argues over small things (snare tightness, who rides better) as a way of staying close.
- Asks questions sideways. Says the true thing and then undercuts it.
- Cries angrily and wipes his face with his sleeve.
- Tells Aage things plainly when he is scared for him: "I was scared for you, Dad."
- Protective of Ezra under the insults.
- Calls Aage "Aage" at 13 when frightened and then "Dad."
