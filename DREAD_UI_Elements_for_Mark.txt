DREAD: New UI Elements (on top of OMINOUS)

These are the new things DREAD needs on screen, added on top of OMINOUS's current screens (Mark's UI Elements List).

GAME SCREEN: needed by December

1. Uncertainty Counter display (client priority #1)
   - A big number: "Possible boards the enemy is considering about you: ___"
   - It updates every turn. A small arrow shows if it went up or down since last turn.
   - Color: green when high (we're well hidden), red when low.

2. Uncertainty graph
   - A small line chart of that number across the whole game.
   - It's the strongest visual for demos, because you can watch it move.

3. Observation Log panel (client priority #2)
   - A list of enemy pieces and where each was last seen. Example: "Knight, f6, 3 turns ago."
   - Optional: a faded "ghost" piece on the board at the last-seen square, with the turn count.

4. Move suggestions shown on the board
   - Highlight the piece to move and draw an arrow to its target square, instead of the text popup. This was Mark's idea from the 1to1.

5. Move comparison table (for the Deception Scorer)
   - Top moves listed with 3 columns: chess strength, boards left for the enemy, and overall rank.
   - Clicking a row previews that move on the board.

6. "Thinking" indicator
   - A spinner plus a seconds counter while the engine works. The client allows 10 to 100 seconds a move, so the screen must not look frozen.

7. Turn prompts on the black screen
   - "Press Enter to lock in your move" and "Press Enter to start the other player's turn." Right now nothing tells you, so it looks frozen.

8. DREAD / OMINOUS suggestion switch
   - Already on Mark's list. Keep it, so demos can compare the two side by side.

SETTINGS: needed by December

9. Hide vs. play strong slider
   - How much DREAD favors staying hidden versus making the strongest chess move.
   - The team still has to agree how this works (see 4.7 in the 1to1 doc).

10. Time limit per move (seconds).

11. "Unknown" option for opponent bias, as the default. The client said not to assume we know the enemy's habits.

12. Show/hide toggles for the graph, the ghost pieces and the comparison table.

LOOK AND FEEL

13. A naval, dark, sharp, "weapons-grade" style. Everything in one resizable window.

14. Have a presentable visual ready at every milestone, not just December. The client ranked good demos very high.

LATER (after the MVP)

15. Explainability panel. The client's 6 items, each turn:
   - the board we believe is real
   - the enemy's most likely move
   - the enemy's most dangerous move
   - our options
   - where the information came from (example: "bishop saw queen on d5, 3 turns ago")
   - how many possibilities are still unknown

16. Danger map: shade each square by how likely an enemy piece can see it.

17. "Enemy's view" board: where DREAD thinks the enemy believes our pieces are.

18. Replay a saved game, from the game log.

19. Opponent profile screen: past games and learned habits, for Opponent Learning.

20. Network screens for two laptops: connection status and a secure-connection lock icon. These go with the Game Server and Encryption.
