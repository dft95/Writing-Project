# The Shower

The water is warm. Aage stands under it with his eyes closed, letting it run over his shoulders. For the first time in years, he's just washing dirt away. That's all.

He doesn't hear Isaac come in over the sound of the water. He only knows because he feels the small body slip into the shower behind him, ducking under the spray without asking permission.

"Hey," Aage says softly, turning to face him, smiling.

Isaac is small under the water, his hair plastered to his head. Twelve years old and genuinely happy—actually happy, not just relieved to be alive. He grins up at Aage, water dripping off his nose.

"The water's nice," Isaac says, tilting his face up into the spray.

Aage reaches up and washes Isaac's hair slowly, carefully. His hands are gentle. This is nice. This is just... nice. No undertones, no darkness lurking underneath. Isaac's eyes stay on his, but not from fear. Just because he wants to look at him.

Isaac laughs—actually laughs—when Aage gets soap in his own eyes and squints like an idiot trying to rinse it out.

"You look stupid," Isaac says, and it's teasing. It's light. It's the kind of thing kids say to people they like.

"I am stupid," Aage says, and he pulls Isaac under the water with him, letting the spray rinse out the soap. Isaac splutters and laughs harder, pushing against Aage's chest but not pulling away.

They stand there under the warm water, and Isaac just... exists. He's not braced for disaster. His body isn't tight with fear. He's just a kid in a shower with someone he loves, and that's enough.

"This is nice," Isaac says quietly, leaning against Aage's chest.

"Yeah," Aage agrees, wrapping his arms around him. "It really is."

For once, that's the whole truth. No asterisks, no caveats. Just two people who've finally found safe ground, enjoying a simple moment of warmth and closeness.

Isaac tilts his head back and grins at Aage. "Race you to get dry?"

Aage laughs. "You'll slip on the floor."

"So will you, old man."

It's ridiculous and ordinary and perfect.
